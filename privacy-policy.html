Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Clean Shine Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") provides mobile car detailing services in Chandler, AZ and the greater East Valley. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, cleanshinedetailing.com, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book a service through our website, we collect:

  • Your name and phone number, submitted through our quote and booking forms.
  • Basic technical and usage data (such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location) collected automatically through Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, and similar tools.
  • Ad click identifiers (such as a Google Click ID) and campaign source data, so we can measure which ads led to a booking.

We do not collect payment information through the website. Payment is handled in person or by phone once your service is complete.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your quote request or booking.
  • To schedule and provide the detailing service you requested.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising (for example, which Google Ads campaigns lead to real bookings).
  • To improve our website and service offerings.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information with the following third-party services strictly to operate our business:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers the contact/quote form submissions from our website to us.
  • Google (Tag Manager, Ads, Analytics) — helps us measure website traffic and ad performance. Google may use cookies or similar technologies as part of this. You can learn more about Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies, mainly through Google's advertising and tag management tools, to understand site traffic and measure ad conversions. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not work as intended if you do.

Data retention

We retain quote and booking information for as long as needed to provide our services and for reasonable recordkeeping (such as scheduling history and tax purposes), after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by calling or texting us at (480) 799-7248.

Children's privacy

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
